The Game

Project Revelation is a Turn-Based Roguelike in which you play as the Horsemen of the Apocalypse. In this world, the horsemen are former angels who chose to turn their backs on their fellow celestials and instead spend their days trying to prevent the end of the human world. Humans seeing these figures appear over and over in scenes of great misery such as war, plagues, and famines start believing them to be omens of the end of times as we know it.
