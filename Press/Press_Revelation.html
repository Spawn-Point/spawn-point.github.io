Project Revelation

Description

Project Revelation is a Turn-Based Roguelike in which you play as the Horsemen of the Apocalypse. In this world, the horsemen are former angels who chose to turn their backs on their fellow celestials and instead spend their days trying to prevent the end of the human world. Humans seeing these figures appear over and over in scenes of great misery such as war, plagues, and famines start believing them to be omens of the end of times as we know it. The game has you play as these misunderstood horsemen as they attempt to thwart the angelic plans in motion. Choose your path forward, face the celestial beings and rise from Death to stop the eminent Apocalypse.

Key Features

  • Perspective

    Our game features 2D Pixel-Art Characters in 3D environments with Low Poly Style assets.
    We are currently working on a Retro Style Shader for the 3D environments,
    allowing for more cohesion between the art styles of the 3D elements and the 2D ones.

  • Progression

    Dynamically Generated Progression trees for each level with multiple encounter types
    such as capture the point, elimination, and bosses.

  • Build Variety

    Battle it out in your fashion.
    Use different items to obtain abilities and craft your party synergy!

  • Unique storytelling

    Experience a fresh narrative twist as the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse defy expectations,
    stepping into the role of saviors instead of destroyers.
    Battle a world that fears and opposes you as you strive to prevent its end.
