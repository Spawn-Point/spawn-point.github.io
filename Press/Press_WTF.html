What The Factory

Description

WHÅT THE FÅCTÖRY is here to prove that chaos has charm, and trial your ability to manage hazards! In this game, you’re put in the shoes of a recently hired factory overseer, tasked with making sure each shift ends with maximum profit. Made in the Unity Engine, this fast paced strategy and resource management experience will have you clear the way for productivity. From putting out fires, to fixing machines and nudging your distractible task force out of harm’s way.

Key Features

  • Worker AI

    The player manages a factory populated by Worker AI that roams around the level completing tasks and getting themselves in dangerous and maybe deadly situations.

  • Deaths

    The death of workers will cost precious time. Worker-Machine interactions generate money, so the more workers alive at any point the faster the player can rank up their score.

  • Levels

    Three differently themed levels full of machines, each with a unique interaction for player and workers alike. Machine malfunction is common and may lead to the loss of workers..

  • Optional Objectives

    Each level contains optional objectives, rewarding extra points, if the player is able to keep up with all of them. Challenge runs, with unique goals and or complications are also available for those looking to test their management skills.
