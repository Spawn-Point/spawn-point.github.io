The Game

Play as Jack, a supernatural
bounty hunter to rid a
spooky mansion of its ghostly
possession, in this fun Roguelike
top down game

Monsters

When the mansion was invaded
by the supernatural, several of its items
came to life with an undying will to
defend the house, such as paintings,
stools, many suits of armor,.... wait
does that mean there were already
skeletons in the house? Guys?

The Mansion

Explore an ever changing mansion
with a different layour each time,
explore the ruins as you battle your

every door
